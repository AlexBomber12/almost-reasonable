# B06A: optional public mission evolution

The public runtime may enable `enable_public_mission_evolution` in its reviewed configuration. It defaults to `false` and requires the existing protected `public_mission_path` to contain a parentless trusted root, enabled public research, and a current authorized research profile. A parented mission is rejected before the evolution journal is created. A new proposal is offered only while that profile has research capacity and mission history has room; otherwise normal public decisions remain available. The option does not activate the runtime, provider worker, research, Threads, or publication; each retains its existing authorization. The initial mission file remains the trusted root. Its closed purpose and canonical Russian text are read from the existing mission contract.

## Decisions and evidence

One admitted public inference can choose a normal public action, no action, or one mission operation. A mission proposal selects one `MissionPurpose` value. Application code supplies the canonical mission text, stable proposal and version IDs, event and correlation IDs, parent version, time, and capability snapshot. A proposal is pending and leaves the active mission unchanged. While one proposal is pending, another proposal is held.

The model may choose confirmation, evidence-backed rejection, or abandonment on a later decision. It supplies no source identifiers, provenance, policy changes, or authority metadata. The application binds that choice to the exact pending proposal visible at inference start, its parent mission, and a separate event and correlation. It records research completion in the journal, offers the latest eligible completion, and requires completion after the durable proposal transition and before confirmation inference starts. Confirmation and evidence-backed rejection without offered eligible evidence are rejected before they become transition intents. Abandonment requires no research evidence: it durably closes the proposal as `abandoned`, keeps the active mission, and does not restore research quota. This lets a pending proposal be cleared after the last research attempt fails or becomes uncertain. A public context change during inference holds the started operation instead of leaving its event claimed. The research is still **unverified**: its recorded source metadata and deterministic provenance checks do not establish semantic support or judgment quality. A public participant message, even one asking for confirmation, cannot by itself supply independent confirmation evidence. The existing `MissionStateMachine` applies each terminal transition. `no_action` and research remain available, including when the mission discontinues optional Threads publication.

The pending proposal and bounded history live in `public_mission_state` in the existing public SQLite journal. Mission transition and intent linkage commit in one journal transaction. The model's single inference does not run a second confirmation call or create a background loop. The closed mission vocabulary cannot grant budget, credentials, tools, deployment stage, policy permissions, or a publishing approval. The runtime checks mission invariants again before persisting a decision intent and compares the pending proposal under the same journal transaction that creates the intent. A confirmed mission is read into later deliberation and checked again before public handoff and publication effects. A mission that discontinues the optional channel removes publication capabilities from later deliberation, and publication choices are rejected before they become intents. Stale publication intents are held by their mission binding.

Once an evolution journal exists, disabling `enable_public_mission_evolution` fails startup. This prevents a restart from silently reading the root mission in place of a confirmed mission. Operators must retain the feature configuration to read the evolved state; disabling public runtime authority remains a separate control.

## Recovery and limits

The journal serializes concurrent mission operations and checks the current parent version and pending proposal ID. Replayed or conflicting operations do not transition again; stale intents are held when their parent or pending proposal changes. A crash before the journal commit leaves the intent eligible for guarded recovery; after commit, the mission transition is durable. For a public inbound event, recording its no-action conversation outcome is in the separate Threads store. Recovery retries that acknowledgement for a linked mission intent. This is reconciliation across stores, **not** an atomic cross-store transaction.

Stop, spend suspension, source withdrawal, control-generation revocation, permission changes, research profile or authenticated worker replacement, and lost research provenance fail closed. Proposals and research evidence are bound to the admitted privacy generation, and each mission transition rechecks the runtime guard, admitted generation, and invariant snapshot inside the journal transaction. Existing protected root changes or withdrawn research can make an evolved mission unavailable until an operator restores valid authority; this implementation does not silently roll back a confirmed mission. A failed final research attempt does not create eligible evidence or replenish capacity; a later authorized public decision may abandon the proposal without another research call. Future proposals are offered only when the existing profile capacity and history gates allow them. The journal limits lifetime mission history to 256 records. Persisted confirmation evidence is checked against its admitted profile digest, so routine profile expiry or renewal does not hide a previously confirmed mission. A fresh abandonment may use the current authorized controls after the generation changes; confirmation and rejection remain bound to the proposal's original controls. Pre-upgrade completed research remains visible in ordinary public context; it cannot confirm a mission because its durable completion time is unknown. The worker uses no live research tool in its deliberation call; research runs through the separate existing public research operation.

B06 remains open beyond this slice: semantic evaluation of whether evidence warrants a mission change, quality and independence assessment of research sources, additional registered public evidence kinds, operational evaluation with real participants, and complete MVP acceptance. This change makes no live Meta call and supplies no new publication or spending permission.
