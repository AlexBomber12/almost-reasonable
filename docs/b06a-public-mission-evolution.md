# B06A: optional public mission evolution

The public runtime may enable `enable_public_mission_evolution` in its reviewed configuration. It defaults to `false` and requires the existing protected `public_mission_path`, enabled public research, and a current authorized research profile. A new proposal is offered only while that profile has research capacity; otherwise normal public decisions remain available. The option does not activate the runtime, provider worker, research, Threads, or publication; each retains its existing authorization. The initial mission file remains the trusted root. Its closed purpose and canonical Russian text are read from the existing mission contract.

## Decisions and evidence

One admitted public inference can choose a normal public action, no action, or one mission operation. A mission proposal selects one `MissionPurpose` value. Application code supplies the canonical mission text, stable proposal and version IDs, event and correlation IDs, parent version, time, and capability snapshot. A proposal is pending and leaves the active mission unchanged. While one proposal is pending, another proposal is held.

The model may choose confirmation or rejection on a later decision. It supplies no source identifiers, provenance, policy changes, or authority metadata. The application requires a separate event and correlation and completed public research recorded after the proposal. The research is still **unverified**: its recorded source metadata and deterministic provenance checks do not establish semantic support or judgment quality. A public participant message, even one asking for confirmation, cannot by itself supply independent confirmation evidence. The existing `MissionStateMachine` applies the final transition. Rejection retains the active mission. `no_action` and research remain available, including when the mission discontinues optional Threads publication.

The pending proposal and bounded history live in `public_mission_state` in the existing public SQLite journal. Mission transition and intent linkage commit in one journal transaction. The model's single inference does not run a second confirmation call or create a background loop. The closed mission vocabulary cannot grant budget, credentials, tools, deployment stage, policy permissions, or a publishing approval. A confirmed mission is read into later deliberation and checked again before public handoff and publication effects. Stale publication intents are held by their mission binding.

Once an evolution journal exists, disabling `enable_public_mission_evolution` fails startup. This prevents a restart from silently reading the root mission in place of a confirmed mission. Operators must retain the feature configuration to read the evolved state; disabling public runtime authority remains a separate control.

## Recovery and limits

The journal serializes concurrent mission operations and checks the current parent version. Replayed or conflicting operations do not transition again. A crash before the journal commit leaves the intent eligible for guarded recovery; after commit, the mission transition is durable. For a public inbound event, recording its no-action conversation outcome is in the separate Threads store. Recovery retries that acknowledgement for a linked mission intent. This is reconciliation across stores, **not** an atomic cross-store transaction.

Stop, spend suspension, source withdrawal, permission changes, and lost research provenance fail closed. Existing protected root changes or withdrawn research can make an evolved mission unavailable until an operator restores valid authority; this implementation does not silently roll back a confirmed mission. The journal limits lifetime mission history to 256 records. The research profile must remain available for persisted confirmation evidence checks. The worker uses no live research tool in its deliberation call; research runs through the separate existing public research operation.

B06 remains open beyond this slice: semantic evaluation of whether evidence warrants a mission change, quality and independence assessment of research sources, additional registered public evidence kinds, operational evaluation with real participants, and complete MVP acceptance. This change makes no live Meta call and supplies no new publication or spending permission.
